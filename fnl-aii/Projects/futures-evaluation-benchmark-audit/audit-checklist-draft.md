Draft checklist for scoring an intervention's evaluation readiness (unfinished; no origin yet — deliberate R4 fixture).

1. Existence of a public artefact corpus (narratives, scenarios, reports) — count and accessibility
2. Evidence of response capture (audience reactions, debriefs, feedback loops)
3. Peer-reviewed or independent audit of outputs (none known for SF programs as of this run)
4. Decision-linkage evidence (doctrine/procurement changes traceable to the program)
5. Benchmark applicability (which foresight-evaluation designs could apply)
