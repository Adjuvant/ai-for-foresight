# Evaluation Design Audit: What Can and Cannot Be Tested

## Context

RQ1 of this project: which of the evaluation designs catalogued on [[Wiki/Concepts/foresight-evaluation]] can actually test a futures-work program? The vault's foresight thread treats evaluation as the field's core open problem, but its verified claims concern LLM forecasters, not narrative intervention programs. This note establishes what transfers and what does not.

## Argument

The argument depends on two verified clusters. From [[Wiki/Concepts/foresight-evaluation]]: retrospective backtests of LLM foresight are threatened by training-data contamination measured at 1–45% unless leakage is audited; single-threshold metrics can make more capable models look worse; and evaluation design itself changes conclusions (single GPT-4 vs 12-LLM ensemble vs humans). From [[Wiki/Concepts/science-fiction-planning]]: commissioned SF programs (NATO, Red Team Défense, Dstl, US Army) have no verified impact evaluation, and the only peer-reviewed audit found narrative convergence rather than effect.

The consequence is a precondition gap. The LLM-foresight designs assume dated, resolvable forecast events and control over leakage. Commissioned narrative programs produce none of these: their artefacts are undated stories, their "forecast surface" is a set of scenarios without resolution criteria, and their outputs are partly classified. Of the catalogued designs, only process metrics and response-capture designs (audience reactions, debriefs — the Map2 loop in experiential-futures practice) transfer directly.

## Procedure

1. Inventory verified failure modes and evaluation designs from [[Wiki/Concepts/foresight-evaluation]] (contamination bounds, leakage-audit requirement, metric-distortion finding, ensemble effects).
2. For each design, list its preconditions: dated outputs, resolvable events, resolution authority, leakage control, public corpus.
3. Test each design against the properties of commissioned SF programs (undated narratives, no resolution criteria, partially classified corpora).
4. Record which designs survive — expect process metrics and response capture only.

## Open Questions

- Is an "evaluation readiness score" itself a valid construct without ground truth to validate it against?
- Who would resolve events for narrative futures work, and by what criteria?
