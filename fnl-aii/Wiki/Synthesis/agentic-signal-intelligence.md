# Agentic Signal Intelligence

Convergent landscape: conventional agentic AI systems (model + MCP + browser tools + Deep-Research-class agents) are being repurposed rather than bespoke-built for intelligence gathering, foresight and weak-signal analysis, with differentiation concentrated in tool layers; foresight automation is the least mature layer and evaluation of agentic vs manual scanning is missing everywhere.

## Open Questions

## Key Patterns
1. **Model + MCP + browser as commodity substrate.** Differentiation concentrates in tool/skill layers (OSINT skill packs, MCP servers, evaluation pipelines), not model or architecture [1][3] [[Literature/agentic-signal-intelligence#^agentic-signal-intelligence-1]] [[Literature/agentic-signal-intelligence#^agentic-signal-intelligence-3]].
2. **Deep-Research-class agents are the default 'intelligence gatherer'** — OpenAI/Google/Perplexity deep research and open clones (STORM, gpt-researcher, open_deep_research) now serve as the collection-and-synthesis layer that foresight/policy pilots wrap with source feeds and taxonomies [24][26][29][30][31][32] [[Literature/agentic-signal-intelligence#^agentic-signal-intelligence-24]] [[Literature/agentic-signal-intelligence#^agentic-signal-intelligence-29]] [[Literature/agentic-signal-intelligence#^agentic-signal-intelligence-30]] [[Literature/agentic-signal-intelligence#^agentic-signal-intelligence-31]].
3. **Commercial incumbents absorb agentic features rather than being replaced** — Recorded Future, Dataminr, Perigon, GreyNoise pair new LLM/agent features with existing collection infrastructure [4][5][7][8] [[Literature/agentic-signal-intelligence#^agentic-signal-intelligence-4]] [[Literature/agentic-signal-intelligence#^agentic-signal-intelligence-5]] [[Literature/agentic-signal-intelligence#^agentic-signal-intelligence-7]] [[Literature/agentic-signal-intelligence#^agentic-signal-intelligence-8]].
4. **Governments document foresight programmes, not agentic internals** — scanning and foresight institutionalisation (EU, UK, EMA, UN) is public, but agentic automation inside them appears in passing (GOV.UK AI Insights series) or not at all [20][21][22] [[Literature/agentic-signal-intelligence#^agentic-signal-intelligence-20]] [[Literature/agentic-signal-intelligence#^agentic-signal-intelligence-21]] [[Literature/agentic-signal-intelligence#^agentic-signal-intelligence-22]].
5. **Weak-signal LLM research is belated and thin** — 2025–2026 papers benchmark LLM foresight capability (venture tournaments, technological-foresight hybrids), but no verified deployed 'LLM weak-signal detector' product surfaced [18][19] [[Literature/agentic-signal-intelligence#^agentic-signal-intelligence-18]] [[Literature/agentic-signal-intelligence#^agentic-signal-intelligence-19]].

## Gaps & Open Questions

- No verified dedicated 'LLM weak-signal detection' product or government agentic-foresight deployment with public documentation was found; the 2026 commercial audit reinforces this (all vendor performance claims vendor-reported) and the OECD/WEF survey shows ~2/3 of practitioners use AI without named in-house agentic stacks [19] [[Literature/foresight-automation-weak-signals#^foresight-automation-weak-signals-19]].
- ISO horizon-scanning standards (candidate numbering 27950/56088) could not be verified; still open (also in the deep-dive's Open Questions).
- Evaluation is missing everywhere — *now partially addressed*: the foresight-automation-weak-signals brief synthesises a stacked evaluation design (prospective tournaments, dynamic future-only benchmarks, leakage-audited backtests, calibration, process metrics); see [[Wiki/Concepts/foresight-evaluation]]. Residual: the SSRN tournament numbers are single-source (Crossref-captured abstract) pending SSRN page access.
- Vendor lead-time claims (Nextatlas 30 months vs 3y7m; Shaping Tomorrow 90K/100K/160K sources) remain unaudited by any independent timestamped-corpus study — open verification target.
- Whether van Veen & Ortt's distance-to-frame-of-reference can be computed by LLMs is explicitly open per the authors — the construct's computational analogue does not exist yet.
- Boundary: GCHQ-adjacent press items (air-gapped LLMs for intelligence analysts) are reported, not verified, and out of scope as operational detail.
