# Future Sign

Hiltunen's semiotic reframe of weak signals (Futures, 2008): a 'future sign' has three dimensions — the signal carrier, the underlying issue, and the interpretation — making weakness a joint property across dimensions rather than a property of the information alone; Hiltunen proposed renaming weak signals 'weak future signs'.

## Key Claims
- The future sign maps Peirce's triadic sign model onto weak signals: signal (number/visibility of carriers), issue (number of events/diffusion) and interpretation (receiver's understanding in context); weakness is joint across the three dimensions and increases in strength as any dimension grows [12] [[Literature/foresight-automation-weak-signals#^foresight-automation-weak-signals-12]].
- Hiltunen distinguished two weak-sign regimes — 'early information' (few signals, few events, hard to interpret) and 'first symptoms' (many visible signals, unclear meaning) — which require different detection approaches [12] [[Literature/foresight-automation-weak-signals#^foresight-automation-weak-signals-12]].
- Hiltunen argued the field should replace 'weak signals' with 'weak future signs' because the triadic model clarifies the difference between what is actually happening (issue) and its information value (signal), countering media-driven overvaluation [12] [[Literature/foresight-automation-weak-signals#^foresight-automation-weak-signals-12]].
