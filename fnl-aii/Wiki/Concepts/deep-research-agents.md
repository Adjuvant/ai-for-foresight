# Deep Research Agents

Deep-Research-class agent systems — OpenAI Deep Research, Google Gemini Deep Research, Perplexity Deep Research and open clones (STORM, gpt-researcher, open_deep_research) — have become the default collection-and-synthesis layer that foresight and policy pilots wrap with source feeds and taxonomies.

## Key Claims
- Deep-Research-class agents are the default 'intelligence gatherer': OpenAI, Google and Perplexity deep research plus open clones (STORM, gpt-researcher, open_deep_research) now serve as the collection-and-synthesis layer that foresight and policy pilots wrap with source feeds and taxonomies [24][26][29][30][31] [[Literature/agentic-signal-intelligence#^agentic-signal-intelligence-24]] [[Literature/agentic-signal-intelligence#^agentic-signal-intelligence-26]] [[Literature/agentic-signal-intelligence#^agentic-signal-intelligence-29]] [[Literature/agentic-signal-intelligence#^agentic-signal-intelligence-30]] [[Literature/agentic-signal-intelligence#^agentic-signal-intelligence-31]].
- Differentiation in 'conventional adaptations' concentrates in tool and skill layers (MCP servers, OSINT skill packs, evaluation pipelines) rather than in the underlying model or agent architecture [1][3][33][34][36][37] [[Literature/agentic-signal-intelligence#^agentic-signal-intelligence-1]] [[Literature/agentic-signal-intelligence#^agentic-signal-intelligence-3]] [[Literature/agentic-signal-intelligence#^agentic-signal-intelligence-33]] [[Literature/agentic-signal-intelligence#^agentic-signal-intelligence-34]] [[Literature/agentic-signal-intelligence#^agentic-signal-intelligence-36]] [[Literature/agentic-signal-intelligence#^agentic-signal-intelligence-37]].
- Anthropic's engineering notes document a multi-agent research system as an explicit orchestration pattern for agentic deep research [27] [[Literature/agentic-signal-intelligence#^agentic-signal-intelligence-27]].
