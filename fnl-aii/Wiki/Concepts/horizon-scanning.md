# Horizon Scanning

Systematic scanning of emerging trends and weak signals to enable early response; as of mid-2026 institutional practice (EMA, EU, UK, UN) outpaces verified LLM-horizon-scanning tooling, with most vendor AI claims promotional or unverified.

## Key Claims
- As of mid-2026, purpose-built 'LLM horizon scanning' products with verified capability are rare; the AI claims of trend-signal platforms (Nextatlas, Futures Platform, Shaping Tomorrow) remain largely promotional or unverified [10][12][13] [[Literature/agentic-signal-intelligence#^agentic-signal-intelligence-10]] [[Literature/agentic-signal-intelligence#^agentic-signal-intelligence-12]] [[Literature/agentic-signal-intelligence#^agentic-signal-intelligence-13]].
- Institutional scanning practice is ahead of tooling: the EMA runs a documented medicines horizon-scanning programme (2026 Frontiers in Medicine paper), while EU, UK and UN programmes document scanning practice without detail on agentic AI internals [11][20][21][22] [[Literature/agentic-signal-intelligence#^agentic-signal-intelligence-11]] [[Literature/agentic-signal-intelligence#^agentic-signal-intelligence-20]] [[Literature/agentic-signal-intelligence#^agentic-signal-intelligence-21]] [[Literature/agentic-signal-intelligence#^agentic-signal-intelligence-22]].
- MIT Sloan Management Review's 'Four Keys to Effective Horizon Scanning' is the canonical recent practitioner method text for the field [9] [[Literature/agentic-signal-intelligence#^agentic-signal-intelligence-9]].
- EMA operates the most concretely documented institutional scanning workflow found: scientific literature, funding calls and grey literature screened daily into an internal tool, with an annual expert signal-prioritisation exercise over a 3–10-year horizon [1] [[Literature/foresight-automation-weak-signals#^foresight-automation-weak-signals-1]].
- MIT SMR's 'Four Keys to Effective Horizon Scanning' (curiosity, scientific approach, humility, storytelling) is a behavioural prescription for individual managers rather than an institutional operating system; the organisational layer is supplied by SOIF-style capability building and EMA-style process (inference in the source) [24] [[Literature/foresight-automation-weak-signals#^foresight-automation-weak-signals-24]] [64] [[Literature/foresight-automation-weak-signals#^foresight-automation-weak-signals-64]].
- No independent audit exists for any commercial horizon-scanning performance claim: Nextatlas '30 months early / 93% accuracy', Futures Platform 'no hallucinations', and Shaping Tomorrow source counts are all vendor-reported, with internal inconsistencies surfaced (30 months vs 3y7m; 90K/100K/160K sources) [4] [[Literature/foresight-automation-weak-signals#^foresight-automation-weak-signals-4]] [48] [[Literature/foresight-automation-weak-signals#^foresight-automation-weak-signals-48]] [49] [[Literature/foresight-automation-weak-signals#^foresight-automation-weak-signals-49]] [7] [[Literature/foresight-automation-weak-signals#^foresight-automation-weak-signals-7]] [8] [[Literature/foresight-automation-weak-signals#^foresight-automation-weak-signals-8]] [9] [[Literature/foresight-automation-weak-signals#^foresight-automation-weak-signals-9]].
