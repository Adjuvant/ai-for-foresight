# Policy Foresight

Institutionalised foresight programmes that feed policy and regulation — EU Strategic Foresight, GOV.UK AI Insights, UN Global Pulse — where procurement of commercial intelligence platforms is the de facto channel for agentic-adjacent capability and in-house agentic stacks remain undocumented.

## Key Claims
- EU Commission Strategic Foresight runs a documented horizon-scanning process that feeds strategic planning and the annual Foresight Reports [20] [[Literature/agentic-signal-intelligence#^agentic-signal-intelligence-20]].
- GOV.UK's 'AI Insights' series, including an Agentic AI volume, is the strongest openly documented example of a government scanning AI developments with institutional rigour [21] [[Literature/agentic-signal-intelligence#^agentic-signal-intelligence-21]].
- Public records show foresight institutionalisation across the EU, UK, EMA and UN, but agentic automation inside those programmes is rarely documented; procurement of commercial real-time-intelligence platforms (e.g., Dataminr) is the de facto adoption channel [5][20][21][22] [[Literature/agentic-signal-intelligence#^agentic-signal-intelligence-5]] [[Literature/agentic-signal-intelligence#^agentic-signal-intelligence-20]] [[Literature/agentic-signal-intelligence#^agentic-signal-intelligence-21]] [[Literature/agentic-signal-intelligence#^agentic-signal-intelligence-22]].
- The Bank of England's 2025 Independent Evaluation Office audit found institutional scanning 'very decentralised and heterogenous' with 'no formal Bankwide policy, framework or guidance', issuing nine recommendations including a central coordinating team — evidence that institutional scanning needs explicit governance, not just tools [2] [[Literature/foresight-automation-weak-signals#^foresight-automation-weak-signals-2]].
- UN DESA and the OECD institutionalise the demand side: leaders who do not embed foresight 'risk being repeatedly surprised by changes that could have been anticipated', and discounting plausible futures 'leaves economies and societies vulnerable to unexpected shocks' [3] [[Literature/foresight-automation-weak-signals#^foresight-automation-weak-signals-3]] [23] [[Literature/foresight-automation-weak-signals#^foresight-automation-weak-signals-23]].
- EMA's daily-screening/annual-prioritisation pattern, with published deep-dive reports (targeted protein degradation, radiopharmaceuticals, NAMs, Alzheimer's), functions as a de facto requirement set for any future institutional scanning tool [1] [[Literature/foresight-automation-weak-signals#^foresight-automation-weak-signals-1]].
