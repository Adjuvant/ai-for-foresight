# Stuart Candy

Futurist and originator of experiential futures; co-creator (with Kelly Kornet) of the Ethnographic Experiential Futures (EXF) cycle — Map, Multiply, Mediate, Mount, Map again — which Pink et al. (2023) situate as the step-by-step, embodied strand of ethnographic foresighting.

## Key Texts
| Text | Year | Key Concept |
|------|------|-------------|
| Candy and Kornet (2019) introduced the Ethnographic Experiential Futures (EXF) approach, which brings together futurists, designers and researchers in a structured process of 'Map, Multiply, Mediate, Mount, and Map again', extending critical and participatory foresight work into a deeply embodied mode; Pink et al. (2023) position EXF within the ethnographic foresighting family as a step-by-step approach. | 2023 |  |
| Co-author (with Kelly Kornet) of 'Turning Foresight Inside Out: An Introduction to Ethnographic Experiential Futures' (Journal of Futures Studies, EXF paper); originator of experiential futures as a practice of making futures tangible. | 2025 |  |
