## 1. Executive Summary

Horizon scanning and weak-signal analysis sit in an unusual position as of mid-2026: **demand is institutionalizing faster than verified supply**. Regulators, central banks, and UN-system bodies are formalizing scanning as a recurring duty (EMA's medicines horizon-scanning programme; Bank of England's 2025 evaluation; UN Pact for the Future / DESA policy briefs), while the practitioner method canon (MIT SMR, SOIF, Hines et al. 2019) is behavioral and organizational rather than computational, and the three main commercial tools (Nextatlas, Futures Platform, Shaping Tomorrow) make large capability claims with **no independently audited performance evidence** [1](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/medicine/articles/10.3389/fmed.2026.1800213/full) [2](https://www.bankofengland.co.uk/independent-evaluation-office/ieo-report-june-2025) [3](https://desapublications.un.org/policy-briefs/un-desa-policy-brief-no-191-institutionalizing-foresight-integrating-futures-thinking) [4](https://www.nextatlas.com/) [5](https://www.nextatlas.com/about/faqs) [6](https://www.nextatlas.com/foresight) [7](https://www.shapingtomorrow.com/) [8](https://www.shapingtomorrow.com/overview) [9](https://www.shapingtomorrow.com/methodology) [10](https://www.shapingtomorrow.com/what-is-decision-grade-intelligence).

The theory lineage is strong but definitionally unsettled: Ansoff (1975) defined weak signals as a *state-of-knowledge* ladder for graduated response; Hiltunen (2008) reframed them as a three-dimensional "future sign" (signal / issue / interpretation); van Veen & Ortt (2021) unified 68 definitions around a single construct — *distance to the perceiver's frame of reference* — while documenting that the field's definitional fragmentation makes results incomparable [11](https://www.creaciondeestrategia.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/02/Ansoff_1975.pdf) [12](https://aaltodoc.aalto.fi/server/api/core/bitstreams/f1f2a55a-093a-4fad-ad6f-4d14fd5a2e6b/content) [13](https://repository.tudelft.nl/file/File_0f4b0d0f-fb0d-4256-b3dd-b790f990bf18).

LLM-era adaptation is early and split: automation of extraction/classification works (SCANAR/AIDOC ~62% review reduction at 95% recall; BERTrend noise/weak/strong topic classification; OECD/WEF survey: ~60% of foresight practitioners already use AI for horizon scanning), while *prospective prediction quality* is contested — early single-LLM forecasting failed (GPT-4 ≈ 50% baseline in a live tournament), but fully prospective ranking (SSRN 2026: best LLM 0.74 rank correlation vs humans 0.04–0.45) and contamination-controlled benchmarks (ForecastBench; BLF with <1.5% leakage) show credible paths [14](http://arxiv.org/abs/2310.13014) [15](https://doi.org/10.2139/ssrn.6166986) [16](http://arxiv.org/abs/2409.19839) [17](http://arxiv.org/abs/2604.18576) [18](http://arxiv.org/abs/2504.01627) [19](https://www.oecd.org/content/dam/oecd/en/publications/reports/2025/11/ai-in-strategic-foresight_82c3a4b1/aa573076-en.pdf).

The open problem that organizes the whole field is **evaluation**: no agreed metric exists for "early and right," and the LLM-specific threat is temporal contamination (models trained on future outcomes relative to a frozen scanning date). The most defensible answer is a *stacked* design: prospective pilot tournaments + dynamic future-only benchmarks + chronological backtests with leakage audits + calibration scoring + process metrics, scored with both bounded and unbounded accuracy measures [16](http://arxiv.org/abs/2409.19839) [20](http://arxiv.org/abs/2606.18686) [21](http://arxiv.org/abs/2605.22672) [17](http://arxiv.org/abs/2604.18576) [22](http://arxiv.org/abs/2402.18563).

## 2. Problem Framing: Why This Matters

### 2.1 Why it is needed (demand side)

- **Scanning is becoming an institutional duty, not a discretionary practice.** EMA runs a documented medicines horizon-scanning programme under the EU Medicines Agencies Network Strategy to 2028, screening scientific literature, funding calls and grey literature **daily**, with an **annual** signal-prioritisation exercise, to identify therapies/technological enablers/societal changes on a **3–10-year horizon** [1](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/medicine/articles/10.3389/fmed.2026.1800213/full). The Bank of England's Independent Evaluation Office (June 2025) found its own scanning "very decentralised and heterogenous," with "no formal Bankwide policy, framework or guidance," and issued nine recommendations including a central coordinating team — a concrete instance of institutionalization-then-audit [2](https://www.bankofengland.co.uk/independent-evaluation-office/ieo-report-june-2025). UN DESA Policy Brief No. 191 warns that leaders who do not embed foresight "risk being repeatedly surprised by changes that could have been anticipated" [3](https://desapublications.un.org/policy-briefs/un-desa-policy-brief-no-191-institutionalizing-foresight-integrating-futures-thinking); the OECD states that discounting plausible futures "leaves economies and societies vulnerable to unexpected shocks" [23](https://www.oecd.org/en/about/programmes/strategic-foresight.html).
- **The original motivation is strategic surprise.** Ansoff's 1975 premise: organizations are surprised even when advance forecasts exist — the 1973–74 petroleum crisis "was to be found on the desks of some of the surprised managers" on the day of the surprise [11](https://www.creaciondeestrategia.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/02/Ansoff_1975.pdf). Effective scanning exists to compress the gap between signal availability and organizational response.
- **Human scanning has structural limits.** The MIT SMR "four keys" article (Anthony, 2025) quotes Day & Schoemaker: "The cognitive challenges at the periphery are far greater than in our focal areas since there are less data to work with and more room for bias and distortion" [24](https://sloanreview.mit.edu/article/four-keys-to-effective-horizon-scanning/). Vendor positioning repeats the same gap: Nextatlas markets "foresight your AI can't give you" because "by the time a trend report lands on your desk, the moment has already passed" [4](https://www.nextatlas.com/) [25](https://generate.nextatlas.com/).

### 2.2 How it could be evaluated (the field's core open problem)

Evidence-backed evaluation designs, each with known failure modes:

1. **Fully prospective tournaments** — predictions frozen before outcome resolution, ideally on targets postdating model training cutoffs so memorization is structurally impossible (SSRN 2026 design: 870 pairwise LLM comparisons on 30 post-cutoff ventures, outcomes unknown during evaluation) [15](https://doi.org/10.2139/ssrn.6166986). Gold standard for hindsight-free evidence; slow, one-shot, cannot be re-run on new models (inference).
2. **Dynamic future-only benchmarks** — ForecastBench (~1,000 unanswered questions, refreshed continuously; "data leakage impossible by construction"); expert forecasters beat the top LLM at launch (p<0.001); ForecastBench-Sim uses simulated worlds (Freeciv rollouts) for contamination-free, immediately resolvable questions [16](http://arxiv.org/abs/2409.19839) [20](http://arxiv.org/abs/2606.18686).
3. **Chronological backtests with leakage audits** — retrospective scoring on timestamped resolved events, with explicit contamination checks: the BLF forecaster's framework measures leakage <1.5% [17](http://arxiv.org/abs/2604.18576); contamination-detection methods exist but have "non-trivial limitations" and little cross-method consistency [26](http://arxiv.org/abs/2409.09927) [27](http://arxiv.org/abs/2406.04244) [28](http://arxiv.org/abs/2502.14425) [29](http://arxiv.org/abs/2310.17589) [30](http://arxiv.org/abs/2402.15938) [31](http://arxiv.org/abs/2407.08716) [32](http://arxiv.org/abs/2311.06233).
4. **Calibration scoring** — Brier-score lineage from Tetlock's superforecaster research, carried directly into the LLM literature (Tetlock co-authors several of the LLM-forecasting papers) [33](ISBN 978-0-8041-3669-3; book citation, no URL) [14](http://arxiv.org/abs/2310.13014) [34](http://arxiv.org/abs/2402.19379) [35](http://arxiv.org/abs/2402.07862) [36](http://arxiv.org/abs/2502.05253) [37](http://arxiv.org/abs/2505.17989) [38](http://arxiv.org/abs/2506.01578) [16](http://arxiv.org/abs/2409.19839). Informed by the inverse-scaling result: more capable models make *worse* tail-risk forecasts under single-threshold metrics; continuous unbounded accuracy measures are recommended alongside bounded ones [21](http://arxiv.org/abs/2605.22672).
5. **Process metrics** when outcomes are unavailable (the institutional reality) — coverage breadth, source diversity, cadence, analyst-time saved, cost per scan; the SCANAR/AIDOC results (~62% manual-review reduction at 95% recall) are an example of process-level evidence in institutional scanning [18](http://arxiv.org/abs/2504.01627).
6. **Construct-level checks** — whether a scanner's flags actually satisfy the unified weak-signal construct (distance to perceiver's frame of reference; strategic relevance gating); van Veen & Ortt explicitly call for validation of perceived-distance measurement methods, including computational ones [13](https://repository.tudelft.nl/file/File_0f4b0d0f-fb0d-4256-b3dd-b790f990bf18) [39](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0016328721001464).

**Goal mis-specification caveat:** institutional scanning is frequently *preparedness/sense-making*, not point prediction (EMA frames "regulatory preparedness and resilience"; UN DESA frames "a culture of preparedness"). Purely predictive evaluation can mis-specify the goal — a conceptual point (inference, grounded in [1](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/medicine/articles/10.3389/fmed.2026.1800213/full)/[3](https://desapublications.un.org/policy-briefs/un-desa-policy-brief-no-191-institutionalizing-foresight-integrating-futures-thinking) framing).

### 2.3 Where new value comes from (contribution space)

- **An evaluation-design synthesis** for LLM-assisted scanning (prospective ↔ backtest ↔ calibration ↔ process), grounded in the classical constructs — currently missing from the vault and largely from the literature (evaluation is "missing everywhere," per the original brief's gap list).
- **A provenance-disciplined audit** of the commercial layer: every vendor performance claim was found to be self-reported; the value is a reproducible claim→evidence ledger, not vendor marketing summaries [4](https://www.nextatlas.com/) [5](https://www.nextatlas.com/about/faqs) [6](https://www.nextatlas.com/foresight) [40](https://aithority.com/machine-learning/aithority-interview-with-luca-morena-ceo-of-nextatlas/).
- **Construct-misalignment analysis:** classical definitions assume human interpretive sense-making (Hiltunen's interpretation dimension; vv&o's perceiver's frame), while LLM "detection" is text-pattern correlation with no intrinsic frame-of-reference. Whether that gap matters is answerable with the evidence assembled here (partially answered: BERTrend/SCANAR show pattern-level automation works at process level; SSRN mounting evidence at relative-judgment level) [12](https://aaltodoc.aalto.fi/server/api/core/bitstreams/f1f2a55a-093a-4fad-ad6f-4d14fd5a2e6b/content) [13](https://repository.tudelft.nl/file/File_0f4b0d0f-fb0d-4256-b3dd-b790f990bf18) [41](http://arxiv.org/abs/2411.05930) [18](http://arxiv.org/abs/2504.01627) [15](https://doi.org/10.2139/ssrn.6166986).
- **A demand-side map:** institutional practice patterns (EMA cadence/outputs; BoE audit; UN/OECD/WHO rationales; Defra's weak-signals operation) as de facto requirements for any future tool [1](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/medicine/articles/10.3389/fmed.2026.1800213/full) [42](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/weak-signals-and-trend-analysis-horizon-scanning/weak-signals-and-trend-analysis-horizon-scanning) [2](https://www.bankofengland.co.uk/independent-evaluation-office/ieo-report-june-2025) [23](https://www.oecd.org/en/about/programmes/strategic-foresight.html) [43](https://oecd-opsi.org/guide/futures-and-foresight/) [3](https://desapublications.un.org/policy-briefs/un-desa-policy-brief-no-191-institutionalizing-foresight-integrating-futures-thinking) [44](https://www.un.org/sites/un2.un.org/files/our-common-agenda-policy-brief-future-generations-en.pdf) [45](https://www.who.int/activities/who-foresight---monitoring-emerging-technologies-and-building-futures-thinking) [46](https://www.who.int/publications/i/item/9789240044173) [47](https://iris.who.int/server/api/core/bitstreams/4f70ab0e-e6aa-44fa-84da-b6085cf120e1/content).

### 2.4 Why the innovation space is worth watching

- Market pull without verified product: scanning is institutionalizing while no verified "LLM weak-signal detector" product exists [4](https://www.nextatlas.com/) [5](https://www.nextatlas.com/about/faqs) [6](https://www.nextatlas.com/foresight) [41](http://arxiv.org/abs/2411.05930). The evaluation bottleneck is the entry barrier, so credible evaluation capability shapes the market (inference).
- Claim–evidence delta is wide and widening: Nextatlas "93% accuracy" and "avg 30 months early" vs its own blog study reporting ~3y7m under a different denominator, with no third-party audit [4](https://www.nextatlas.com/) [48](https://blog.nextatlas.com/article/listening-to-innovators-checking-with-the-crowd-the-value-and-limits-of-validating-foresight-luca-morena); Futures Platform "No hallucinations" chatbot claim [49](https://www.futuresplatform.com/ai-futurist); Shaping Tomorrow's scan-universe figures vary 90K/100K/160K across its own pages [8](https://www.shapingtomorrow.com/overview) [9](https://www.shapingtomorrow.com/methodology).
- Conceptual ferment: the weak-signal construct is still not settled (2021 unification attempt; Hiltunen's semiotic reframe), while LLM behavior adds a new interpretive layer — live conceptual surface, not just an engineering problem [12](https://aaltodoc.aalto.fi/server/api/core/bitstreams/f1f2a55a-093a-4fad-ad6f-4d14fd5a2e6b/content) [13](https://repository.tudelft.nl/file/File_0f4b0d0f-fb0d-4256-b3dd-b790f990bf18).

### 2.5 Current challenges in the field (evidence-backed)

1. **Definitional ambiguity / construct validity** — "research results regarding weak signals cannot be compared once the definitions differ fundamentally" (vv&o 2021); 68 definitions, six clusters, four weakness levels [39](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0016328721001464) [13](https://repository.tudelft.nl/file/File_0f4b0d0f-fb0d-4256-b3dd-b790f990bf18).
2. **Evaluation & hindsight** — no agreed lead-time/calibration metrics; the strongest fully prospective comparison using *structurally post-training-cutoff targets* (where outcome memorization is impossible) is the 2026 SSRN tournament [15](https://doi.org/10.2139/ssrn.6166986); earlier live competitions (e.g., the 2023 Metaculus tournament [14](http://arxiv.org/abs/2310.13014)) were fully prospective but in-distribution.
3. **Noise-adjacency** — weak signals are near-noise by definition; separating them without ground truth [11](https://www.creaciondeestrategia.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/02/Ansoff_1975.pdf) [24](https://sloanreview.mit.edu/article/four-keys-to-effective-horizon-scanning/).
4. **Temporal contamination** — LLM training data include future outcomes relative to any frozen scan date; contamination measured at 1–45% across benchmarks, inflating accuracy up to +14% [29](http://arxiv.org/abs/2310.17589).
5. **Trust/interpretability** — institutional users need to know *why* a flag was raised; none of the surveyed vendor tools documents an interpretability or audit standard [4](https://www.nextatlas.com/) [5](https://www.nextatlas.com/about/faqs) [6](https://www.nextatlas.com/foresight) [50](https://www.futuresplatform.com/) [51](https://www.futuresplatform.com/scenario-analysis-planning-tool) [52](https://www.futuresplatform.com/solution-overview-features) [49](https://www.futuresplatform.com/ai-futurist) [53](https://www.futuresplatform.com/synapse).
6. **Institutional friction** — procurement, liability, and absent public documentation of agentic internals inside government scanning (original brief's finding, confirmed: UK GOV.UK AI Insights is the strongest documented example, and it does not describe agentic internals) [vault][54](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/ai-insights/ai-insights-agentic-ai-html).
7. **AI-washing** — capability claims without standardized disclosure; user reviews are scarce (no independent reviews found at all for Shaping Tomorrow; Futures Platform only on Software Advice; Nextatlas only low-quality aggregators) [55](https://allbestapps.net/ai-app/nextatlas/) [56](https://www.softwareadvice.com/project-management/futures-platform-profile/) [57](https://www.cbinsights.com/company/shaping-tomorrow).

### 2.6 Coupling of challenges to technical & conceptual problems

- **Technical:** LLM confabulation can fabricate weak signals (EPRS notes hallucinations can be *creatively useful* in scenario work but must be human-gated) [58](<https://www.europarl.europa.eu/RegData/etudes/BRIE/2025/774665/EPRS_BRI(2025)774665_EN.pdf>); temporal contamination breaks naive backtests [27](http://arxiv.org/abs/2406.04244) [28](http://arxiv.org/abs/2502.14425) [29](http://arxiv.org/abs/2310.17589) [30](http://arxiv.org/abs/2402.15938) [31](http://arxiv.org/abs/2407.08716) [32](http://arxiv.org/abs/2311.06233); calibration for low-confidence flags is unsolved and capability-scaled (inverse tail-risk scaling) [21](http://arxiv.org/abs/2605.22672); continuous multilingual scanning at institutional cost/throughput limits is unproven; prompt engineering alone gives negligible gains [38](http://arxiv.org/abs/2506.01578).
- **Conceptual:** the detection object itself is undefined — 68 definitions [13](https://repository.tudelft.nl/file/File_0f4b0d0f-fb0d-4256-b3dd-b790f990bf18); LLMs collapse Hiltunen's signal→issue→interpretation distance, and vv&o's "frame of reference" has no obvious computational analogue (they call for validation of distance-measurement methods — computational distance measurement is an open research direction they explicitly flag) [12](https://aaltodoc.aalto.fi/server/api/core/bitstreams/f1f2a55a-093a-4fad-ad6f-4d14fd5a2e6b/content) [13](https://repository.tudelft.nl/file/File_0f4b0d0f-fb0d-4256-b3dd-b790f990bf18); foresight-as-preparedness vs foresight-as-prediction mis-specifies evaluation goals [1](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/medicine/articles/10.3389/fmed.2026.1800213/full) [3](https://desapublications.un.org/policy-briefs/un-desa-policy-brief-no-191-institutionalizing-foresight-integrating-futures-thinking); and the anthropomorphism question (does an LLM "sense" a weak signal or match its textual correlates?) is live in the empirical record: pattern-level classification works (BERTrend), meaning-level judgment is promising but contested (GPT-4 failed at live probabilistic forecasting; Gemini-class models succeeded at relative venture ranking) [41](http://arxiv.org/abs/2411.05930) [14](http://arxiv.org/abs/2310.13014) [15](https://doi.org/10.2139/ssrn.6166986).

## 3. Q1 — Practitioner Method & Institutional Practice

### 3.1 MIT SMR, "Four Keys to Effective Horizon Scanning" (Anthony, 2025)

Full text read [24](https://sloanreview.mit.edu/article/four-keys-to-effective-horizon-scanning/):
- **Publication:** MIT Sloan Management Review column, 6 March 2025; author Scott D. Anthony (Tuck School clinical professor; former Innosight managing partner emeritus) [24](https://sloanreview.mit.edu/article/four-keys-to-effective-horizon-scanning/) [59](https://tuck.dartmouth.edu/faculty/faculty-directory/scott-d-anthony).
- **The four keys (verbatim headings):** 1) **Curiosity** — experiential engagement with emerging technology ("report from the future"; Amazon/Twitch $970M example; "bet on demographics, but hedge on systems"); 2) **A Scientific Approach** — anchored on Tetlock & Gardner's *Superforecasting* and the Good Judgment Project: decompose problems, distinguish knowns from unknowns, use multiple data sources, update forecasts; 3) **Humility** — "the worst predictions of the future often come from people with expertise" (Christensen's iPhone prediction as cautionary tale); Day & Schoemaker's peripheral-vision weak-signal framing; 4) **Storytelling Ability** — JFK's moon-shot speech; Johansen: "Our brains are wired for stories…"; "It's one thing for an individual to have insight about the future. It's another to compel a group to act" [24](https://sloanreview.mit.edu/article/four-keys-to-effective-horizon-scanning/).
- **Frame:** adopts Bob Johansen's definition — horizon scanning = "the effort to turn foresight into insight, and insight into action" [24](https://sloanreview.mit.edu/article/four-keys-to-effective-horizon-scanning/).
- **Limits of the piece (inference):** it is a *behavioral* prescription for individual managers, not an institutional operating system (no source lists, cadence, ownership, or triage rules). The institutional complement lives in adjacent MIT SMR work (Day & Schoemaker's four-step vigilant-company scanning; the Bank of England case) and in the institutional record (EMA, SOIF, BoE IEO) [60](https://sloanreview.mit.edu/article/how-vigilant-companies-gain-an-edge-in-turbulent-times/) [61](https://sloanreview.mit.edu/article/scenario-planning-example-bank-of-england/) [1](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/medicine/articles/10.3389/fmed.2026.1800213/full) [2](https://www.bankofengland.co.uk/independent-evaluation-office/ieo-report-june-2025). No scholarly citations or published critiques of the article were found; absence of found critiques ≠ no critiques exists (needs follow-up) [24](https://sloanreview.mit.edu/article/four-keys-to-effective-horizon-scanning/).

### 3.2 SOIF — School of International Futures

- **Who:** global non-profit founded 2012, UK HQ; mission "help people transform the present using structured thinking about the future"; three disciplines (foresight & futures; participation & governance; networks of change); clients include governments, foundations, civil society [62](https://www.soif.org.uk/) [63](https://soif.org.uk/about-us/).
- **Signals-literate method (verified):** four-stage process — "scoping and horizon scanning, to ordering and sense-making, to identifying implications and integrating outcomes into processes and systems"; prescriptive about the journey, agnostic about tools [64](https://soif.org.uk/foresight-services/). Horizon scanning defined in the SOIF Primer as systematic examination of "threats, opportunities and likely developments… at the margins of current thinking," identifying "weak signals, trends, wild cards" [65](https://cdn2.hubspot.net/hubfs/5401550/SOIF2019+Primer.pdf). Toolkit guidance: STEEP radars, futures wheels, driver analysis; cadence guidance ("One scan per scanner per week"; bursts of several weeks) [66](https://soif.org.uk/app/uploads/2024/01/Toolkit-Strategic-Foresight-Decision-Makers-compressed.pdf) [67](https://soif.org.uk/app/uploads/2025/06/Foresight-Toolkit-Policy-Educators.pdf).
- **Three Horizons lineage (independently verified):** from McKinsey's *Alchemy of Growth*, adapted by Sharpe & Hodgson for the UK Foresight programme's Intelligent Infrastructure Futures project (2006); formal peer-reviewed statement: Sharpe, Hodgson, Leicester, Lyon & Fazey, *Ecology and Society* 21(2):47 (2016); Curry & Hodgson synthesized it for futurists (JFS 2008) [68](https://www.internationalfuturesforum.com/three-horizons) [69](https://ecologyandsociety.org/vol21/iss2/art47/) [70](https://jfsdigital.org/articles-and-essays/2008-2/vol-13-no-1-august/articles/seeing-in-multiple-horizons-connecting-futures-to-strategy/). SOIF Director of Futures Andrew Curry is the JFS co-author [70](https://jfsdigital.org/articles-and-essays/2008-2/vol-13-no-1-august/articles/seeing-in-multiple-horizons-connecting-futures-to-strategy/) [71](https://soif.org.uk/blog/from-disruption-to-transformation/).
- **Teaching/outputs:** five-day Summer Foresight Retreat (2026 paused; next edition 2027, 15th anniversary) [72](https://soif.org.uk/retreat/); *Beyond Strategic Planning* toolkit (2024) [66](https://soif.org.uk/app/uploads/2024/01/Toolkit-Strategic-Foresight-Decision-Makers-compressed.pdf); *Foresight Toolkit for Policy Educators* (2025) [67](https://soif.org.uk/app/uploads/2025/06/Foresight-Toolkit-Policy-Educators.pdf); *Implementation Handbook for the UN Declaration on Future Generations* (2024) [73](https://soif.org.uk/app/uploads/2024/09/SOIF_DFG_Implementation_Handbook_Sept-2024.pdf); UNESCO-aligned futures-literacy programming [74](https://www.unesco.org/en/futures-literacy).
- **Positioning for this study:** SOIF is the practice-side complement to the four-keys article — it converts individual scanning dispositions into organizational capability (which is precisely the layer EMA institutionalizes and BoE's audit found missing) [64](https://soif.org.uk/foresight-services/) [2](https://www.bankofengland.co.uk/independent-evaluation-office/ieo-report-june-2025).

## 5. Q3 — Weak-Signal Theory Lineage

### 5.1 Ansoff (1975) — weak signals as graduated response to strategic surprise

Full text read (public mirror; publisher paywalled) [11](https://www.creaciondeestrategia.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/02/Ansoff_1975.pdf):
- **Core argument:** strategic surprises are "sudden, urgent, unfamiliar changes in the firm's perspective which threaten either a major profit reversal or loss of a major opportunity"; planning systems fail because inputs must be simultaneously early *and* content-rich, which discontinuous threats cannot provide — "if the firm waits until information is adequate for strategic planning, it will be increasingly surprised by crises" [11](https://www.creaciondeestrategia.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/02/Ansoff_1975.pdf).
- **The fix:** invert the fit — design response to the *information state*: "graduated response through amplification and response to weak signals, in contrast to conventional strategic planning that depends on strong signals" [11](https://www.creaciondeestrategia.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/02/Ansoff_1975.pdf).
- **Five states of knowledge (response ladder):** 1) sense of threat/opportunity; 2) source identified; 3) threat/opportunity concrete; 4) response concrete; 5) outcome concrete [11](https://www.creaciondeestrategia.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/02/Ansoff_1975.pdf).
- **Six response strategies** (3 strengths × 2 domains): environment (awareness → external flexibility → direct response) and internal configuration (self-awareness → internal flexibility → readiness); awareness measures are feasible even at the lowest knowledge state: "awareness to flexibility to direct response — enables the firm to start response much earlier… utilizing weak signals" [11](https://www.creaciondeestrategia.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/02/Ansoff_1975.pdf).
- **Operational machinery:** strategic issue list; strategic business areas (SBAs) with per-issue estimates of magnitude, timing, and present state of knowledge; Delphi/judgment in low states, quantitative modeling in higher states; opportunity–vulnerability profiling; **strategic issue management (SIM)** — continuous real-time surveillance, periodic + event-triggered list updates, split responsibility (staff detect/analyze, general management owns priorities), dual feedback; SIM "is a complement, rather than a replacement for strategic planning" (strong signals ↔ planning; weak signals ↔ SIM) [11](https://www.creaciondeestrategia.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/02/Ansoff_1975.pdf).
- **Correction to common attributions:** the famous observation/cognitive/power "filters" do **not** appear in the 1975 CMR text; Hiltunen attributes them to Ansoff's *Implanting Strategic Management* (1984) and vv&o to *Strategic Management* (1979) [12](https://aaltodoc.aalto.fi/server/api/core/bitstreams/f1f2a55a-093a-4fad-ad6f-4d14fd5a2e6b/content) [13](https://repository.tudelft.nl/file/File_0f4b0d0f-fb0d-4256-b3dd-b790f990bf18). Treat filter-citations as Ansoff-later-works [REPORTED][109](https://www.dpublication.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/12/36-636.pdf).

### 5.2 Hiltunen (2008) — the future sign and its three dimensions

Full text read (2010 dissertation reprint; publisher paywalled) [12](https://aaltodoc.aalto.fi/server/api/core/bitstreams/f1f2a55a-093a-4fad-ad6f-4d14fd5a2e6b/content):
- **Problem:** "confusion about what weak signals actually are"; parallel concepts (emerging issues, seeds of change, wild cards, early warning) used as synonyms [12](https://aaltodoc.aalto.fi/server/api/core/bitstreams/f1f2a55a-093a-4fad-ad6f-4d14fd5a2e6b/content).
- **Reframe:** the **future sign**, built on Peirce's triadic sign model: **signal** (representamen; "the number and/or visibility of signals" — the carrier), **issue** (object; "the number of events… diffusion of the phenomenon"), **interpretation** (interpretant; "the receiver's understanding of the future sign's meaning… contexts are included") [12](https://aaltodoc.aalto.fi/server/api/core/bitstreams/f1f2a55a-093a-4fad-ad6f-4d14fd5a2e6b/content).
- **Weakness is joint, in 3D:** "as one dimension of the future sign increases, the strength of the sign increases"; the model explicitly does not promise exact measurement [12](https://aaltodoc.aalto.fi/server/api/core/bitstreams/f1f2a55a-093a-4fad-ad6f-4d14fd5a2e6b/content).
- **Two weak-sign regimes:** *early information* (few signals + few events ⇒ hard interpretation) vs *first symptoms* (many visible signals, unclear meaning) — different detection problems [12](https://aaltodoc.aalto.fi/server/api/core/bitstreams/f1f2a55a-093a-4fad-ad6f-4d14fd5a2e6b/content).
- **Why "future signs":** "I have partly abandoned the term signal because of its problematic nature… I prefer to name weak signals as weak future signs, the weakness of which can be determined with the three dimensions" [12](https://aaltodoc.aalto.fi/server/api/core/bitstreams/f1f2a55a-093a-4fad-ad6f-4d14fd5a2e6b/content).
- **Monograph anchor:** doctoral dissertation *Weak Signals in Organizational Futures Learning* (Helsinki School of Economics/Aalto, Acta A-365, 2010) contains the article reprint; follow-up: Kuusi & Hiltunen (2011, JFS 16(1)) on the signification process [110](https://epub.lib.aalto.fi/en/diss/?cmd=show&dissid=400) [111](https://jfsdigital.org/articles-and-essays/2011-2/vol-16-no-1-september/articles/the-signification-process-of-the-future-sign/).

### 5.3 van Veen & Ortt (2021) — unifying weak-signal definitions

Full text read (CC-BY, TU Delft) [39](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0016328721001464) [13](https://repository.tudelft.nl/file/File_0f4b0d0f-fb0d-4256-b3dd-b790f990bf18):
- **Method:** literature review across foresight, sense-making and entrepreneurial-alertness literatures; 152 papers collected, **68** gave specific weak-signal descriptions; keyword decomposition + hierarchical cluster analysis (no discipline effect; six significant clusters) [39](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0016328721001464) [13](https://repository.tudelft.nl/file/File_0f4b0d0f-fb0d-4256-b3dd-b790f990bf18).
- **Unified definition (verbatim):** "A weak signal is **A perception of strategic phenomena detected in the environment or created during interpretation that are distant to the perceiver's frame of reference**" [39](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0016328721001464) [13](https://repository.tudelft.nl/file/File_0f4b0d0f-fb0d-4256-b3dd-b790f990bf18).
- **Key moves:** weakness = distance to frame of reference (replacing "novel/ambiguous/ill-defined"); novelty, strategic relevance, impact unpredictability are *conditions*, not the essence ("the essence of weakness is the lack of knowledge of the perceiver about a signal"); noise becomes signal only when perceived; a signal persists only if strategically relevant; **four graded weakness levels** from fully-outside-focus to assessed-but-uncertain; explicitly engages Hiltunen's three-part model [39](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0016328721001464) [13](https://repository.tudelft.nl/file/File_0f4b0d0f-fb0d-4256-b3dd-b790f990bf18).
- **Critiques flagged (conceptual challenges):** definition proliferation makes results incomparable; construct ambiguity (field "still busy defining its constructs"); **emergence bias** — words like "peripheral"/"outside frame" appear only post-2004, implying most studies operationalized "weak" as merely "less strong" (bias toward stronger signals); **empirical attribution problem** — perceived weakness is rarely measured, so "weak signals" in studies may not be weak to participants; manager self-report bias on novelty; distance framing fixes these and turns weakness into a decision parameter (larger distance ⇒ less dense, more abstract information) [39](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0016328721001464) [13](https://repository.tudelft.nl/file/File_0f4b0d0f-fb0d-4256-b3dd-b790f990bf18).

### 5.4 Operationalization synthesis (inference)

The three constructs form a lineage: Ansoff makes weakness a **property of the information state** (what do you know, and when is it actionable); Hiltunen makes it a **joint property of carrier–phenomenon–interpretation** with observer subjectivity; vv&o make it a **measurable relational property** (distance to a perceiver's frame). For an automated scanner: Ansoff implies state-labeling + graduated response portfolios; Hiltunen implies three-track representation (carrier count, event diffusion, interpretation with context) and two regime-specific detectors; vv&o implies an explicit, machine-readable frame-of-reference with distance metrics and relevance gating — the requirement LLM systems are least equipped to satisfy today, and the direction vv&o themselves flag for validation research [12](https://aaltodoc.aalto.fi/server/api/core/bitstreams/f1f2a55a-093a-4fad-ad6f-4d14fd5a2e6b/content) [13](https://repository.tudelft.nl/file/File_0f4b0d0f-fb0d-4256-b3dd-b790f990bf18).

- **Design (verified from abstract):** **fully prospective** — 30 U.S. technology ventures **launched after the training cutoffs of all models studied**, evaluated while fundraising was in progress and outcomes were unknown; 870 pairwise LLM comparisons producing complete rankings (a **ranking** task, not probabilistic calibration); baselines = 346 experienced managers (Prolific) + 3 MBA-trained investors + crowd ensembles + human–AI hybrids [15](https://doi.org/10.2139/ssrn.6166986).
- **Results (verified from abstract):** human rank correlations with actual outcomes 0.04–0.45; several frontier LLMs >0.60; best (Gemini 2.5 Pro) **0.74** — "correctly ordering nearly four of every five venture pairs"; differences persist across metrics/robustness checks; neither crowd ensembles nor human–AI hybrids beat the best standalone model [15](https://doi.org/10.2139/ssrn.6166986).
- **Status:** author-stated limitations not readable (SSRN bot-blocked; Crossref abstract captured) [15](https://doi.org/10.2139/ssrn.6166986) [115](https://openalex.org/W7134926118).
- **Design nuance (inference):** a ranking result does not license claims about calibrated probability quality; transfer to institutional scanning should respect that distinction.

- **Classification/labeling:** Stoliar & Savastiyanov (2023), "Using LLM classification in foresight studies," InterConf 157:367–375 — **no DOI** (consistent with the vault's earlier finding); GPT-models for classification in foresight/scenario work, predictions beyond corpus [116](https://archive.interconf.center/index.php/conference-proceeding/article/view/3813). Burmaoglu, Saritas & Kara (2026, Research Square preprint rs-9166858/v1) — LLM (Qwen2.5-3B-Instruct) as a *calibration agent* inside patentometric foresight: resolves 38.9% of rule-layer unclassified observations; ΔH +0.298 bits [118](https://doi.org/10.21203/rs.3.rs-9166858/v1).
- **Weak-signal/early-warning:** **BERTrend** (arXiv:2411.05930) — online neural topic model whose metric "classifies topics as noise, weak, or strong signals," flagging emerging rapidly growing topics; LLMs used for trend interpretability [41](http://arxiv.org/abs/2411.05930). **Khanbayov & Kurban** (arXiv:2606.12828) — early-warning signature for topic phase transitions in AI research, frozen 2017–2021 and tested out-of-sample 2023–2025: precision 27%, recall 63% vs 13.5% base rate; currently flags reasoning/test-time compute, agentic AI, RAG, world models for 2026–2028 [119](http://arxiv.org/abs/2606.12828). ### 6.4 Evaluation literature (Q6 evidence)

- **Single-LLM forecasting failed early:** Schoenegger & Park (arXiv:2310.13014) — GPT-4 in a live Metaculus tournament (Jul–Oct 2023) "did not significantly differ from the no-information forecasting strategy of assigning a 50% probability to every question" and underperformed the median human crowd [14](http://arxiv.org/abs/2310.13014).
- **Retrieval-augmented systems improved:** Halawi et al. (arXiv:2402.18563) — LM forecasting system on a test set published **after** model cutoffs "nears the crowd aggregate of competitive forecasters, and in some settings surpasses it" [22](http://arxiv.org/abs/2402.18563).
- **Ensembles ≈ crowds:** Schoenegger et al. (arXiv:2402.19379) — ensemble of 12 LLMs statistically indistinguishable from a 925-human crowd over 31 binary questions; human-median conditioning improves GPT-4/Claude 2 by 17–28% [34](http://arxiv.org/abs/2402.19379).
- **LLM as human assistant works:** Schoenegger et al. (arXiv:2402.07862; journal DOI 10.1145/3707649) — N=991: frontier LLM assistants improved human forecast accuracy 24–28% vs control [35](http://arxiv.org/abs/2402.07862).
- **Self-improvement from outcomes:** Turtel et al. — outcome-driven DPO raises 14B models 7–10% (≈GPT-4o level) (arXiv:2502.05253); RLVR-trained 14B matches/surpasses frontier o1 with better calibration, +10% ROI in a Polymarket simulation (arXiv:2505.17989) [36](http://arxiv.org/abs/2502.05253) [37](http://arxiv.org/abs/2505.17989).
- **Prompt engineering is not the lever:** Schoenegger et al. (arXiv:2506.01578) — 38+ prompts across 4 models: negligible gains; Bayesian-reasoning prompts *hurt* accuracy [38](http://arxiv.org/abs/2506.01578).
- **Contamination-controlled benchmarks:** **ForecastBench** (arXiv:2409.19839) — ~1,000 future-only questions, no known answer at submission ⇒ leakage impossible by construction; expert forecasters beat top LLM (p<0.001) [16](http://arxiv.org/abs/2409.19839). **ForecastBench-Sim** (arXiv:2606.18686) — Freeciv-rollout simulated world, contamination-free by construction, conditional/causal question families [20](http://arxiv.org/abs/2606.18686). **Inverse scaling** (arXiv:2605.22672) — more capable models make worse tail-risk forecasts under single-threshold metrics; recommends continuous unbounded accuracy measures alongside bounded ones [21](http://arxiv.org/abs/2605.22672). **BLF** (arXiv:2604.18576) — agentic Bayesian linguistic forecaster, SOTA on ForecastBench with a backtesting framework measured at **<1.5% leakage** [17](http://arxiv.org/abs/2604.18576). **AIA Forecaster** (arXiv:2511.07678) — agentic forecaster "equals human superforecasters" on ForecastBench; ensemble with market consensus beats consensus alone [124](http://arxiv.org/abs/2511.07678).
- **Contamination literature:** surveys (arXiv:2406.04244; 2502.14425); open report (arXiv:2310.17589) — contamination 1–45% across six benchmarks, rising over time, up to +14% accuracy inflation; CDD/TED detection+mitigation (arXiv:2402.15938); taxonomy incl. altered test versions (arXiv:2407.08716); black-box contamination quiz (arXiv:2311.06233); and a caution — detection methods have "non-trivial limitations" and little cross-method consistency (arXiv:2409.09927) [27](http://arxiv.org/abs/2406.04244) [28](http://arxiv.org/abs/2502.14425) [29](http://arxiv.org/abs/2310.17589) [30](http://arxiv.org/abs/2402.15938) [31](http://arxiv.org/abs/2407.08716) [32](http://arxiv.org/abs/2311.06233) [26](http://arxiv.org/abs/2409.09927).
- **Anchors:** Tetlock & Gardner, *Superforecasting* (2015) — the calibration-evaluation lineage for predictive judgment [33](ISBN 978-0-8041-3669-3; book citation, no URL).

## 7. Q5/Q6 — Synthesis: Evaluation Design for Institutional Scanning

### 7.1 What transfers (evidence + inference)

1. **Stack the designs, don't choose one** *(recommendation, inference)*: prospective pilot tournament (post-cutoff targets, frozen predictions) + dynamic future-only benchmark + chronological backtest with explicit leakage audit + calibration scoring with bounded *and* unbounded metrics — each covers a failure mode of the others [15](https://doi.org/10.2139/ssrn.6166986) [22](http://arxiv.org/abs/2402.18563) [16](http://arxiv.org/abs/2409.19839) [17](http://arxiv.org/abs/2604.18576) [21](http://arxiv.org/abs/2605.22672).
2. **Leakage is the first-order technical threat** for LLM scanning; measured-leakage reporting (<1.5% standard) should be a publication convention *(recommendation, inference)* [17](http://arxiv.org/abs/2604.18576) [27](http://arxiv.org/abs/2406.04244) [28](http://arxiv.org/abs/2502.14425) [29](http://arxiv.org/abs/2310.17589) [30](http://arxiv.org/abs/2402.15938) [31](http://arxiv.org/abs/2407.08716) [32](http://arxiv.org/abs/2311.06233) [26](http://arxiv.org/abs/2409.09927).
3. **Human-in-the-loop is the current best deployment pattern** *(recommendation, inference)*: LLM ensembles + human review (AIA Forecaster/BLF pattern; SCANAR/AIDOC ~62% review reduction at 95% recall; OECD/WEF: experts report AI weak on inductive/low-probability signals) [124](http://arxiv.org/abs/2511.07678) [17](http://arxiv.org/abs/2604.18576) [18](http://arxiv.org/abs/2504.01627) [19](https://www.oecd.org/content/dam/oecd/en/publications/reports/2025/11/ai-in-strategic-foresight_82c3a4b1/aa573076-en.pdf).
4. **Distinguish ranking from calibration and prediction from preparedness:** the strongest LLM result (0.74 rank correlation) is relative judgment; institutional scanning's stated purpose is often preparedness, which argues for process metrics + scenario-quality evaluation alongside predictive scores [15](https://doi.org/10.2139/ssrn.6166986) [1](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/medicine/articles/10.3389/fmed.2026.1800213/full) [3](https://desapublications.un.org/policy-briefs/un-desa-policy-brief-no-191-institutionalizing-foresight-integrating-futures-thinking) [121](https://doi.org/10.3390/fi17010048) [19](https://www.oecd.org/content/dam/oecd/en/publications/reports/2025/11/ai-in-strategic-foresight_82c3a4b1/aa573076-en.pdf) [58](<https://www.europarl.europa.eu/RegData/etudes/BRIE/2025/774665/EPRS_BRI(2025)774665_EN.pdf>) [122](https://sdgs.un.org/sites/default/files/2024-05/Carlsen_AI%20Assisted%20Scenario%20Building%20for%20Sustainable%20Development.pdf).
5. **Evaluation tension to respect:** "hybrids don't beat best standalone model" (SSRN) vs "LLM assistants improve humans 24–28%" (AI-augmented predictions) are *not* contradictory — they run in opposite augmentation directions (humans assisting a top model vs a model assisting unaided humans) [15](https://doi.org/10.2139/ssrn.6166986) [35](http://arxiv.org/abs/2402.07862). Conflating them is a known misreading to avoid.

### 7.2 Construct-misalignment (inference, grounded)

Classical constructs require an interpreter with a frame of reference (vv&o), contextual sense-making (Hiltunen), and state-of-knowledge honesty (Ansoff). LLM scanners can label and rank but have no intrinsic perceiver-frame; the distance-to-frame metric is exactly the piece that lacks a computational analogue, and vv&o list validation of distance-measurement as the field's next step [12](https://aaltodoc.aalto.fi/server/api/core/bitstreams/f1f2a55a-093a-4fad-ad6f-4d14fd5a2e6b/content) [13](https://repository.tudelft.nl/file/File_0f4b0d0f-fb0d-4256-b3dd-b790f990bf18). Meanwhile pattern-level automation demonstrably works at process level (BERTrend; SCANAR/AIDOC), so the practical near-term is augmentation of analysts, not replacement of interpretation [41](http://arxiv.org/abs/2411.05930) [18](http://arxiv.org/abs/2504.01627).
